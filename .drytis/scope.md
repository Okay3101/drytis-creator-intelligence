# Scope — V1

## In scope (V1)
Creator: email/password/OTP auth; Instagram OAuth (professional accounts only); multiple IG accounts; Existing Content Diagnosis; Content Health (Diagnose-only); Winning Patterns; Reel Readiness + fix/re-upload loop; Trends (personalized, states, Use This Trend); Competitor Intelligence (max 3, quick/full refresh); Script Generator (toggles, revisions, voiceover mode); Analytics; Engagement Automation (25 free/post, plans, follow-gating optional); Marketplace campaign board end-to-end; notifications (push + in-app); support chat; minimal profile/settings; account deletion.

Brand: registration, profile, campaign create (basic+advanced, required vs preferred), publish, application review, approval, workspace (brief, deliverables, content review, chat, payment status label), campaign analytics.

Admin: users, Instagram health, AI config (models/prompts/thresholds), competitors/trends config & moderation, automation plans & limits, coupons, marketplace moderation, notification templates/broadcasts, support inbox, reports, platform settings (audited), analytics dashboard.

## Out of scope (V1)
TikTok/YouTube; auto competitor selection; >3 competitors; AI video/image/voice generation; video editor; creator or brand subscriptions for intelligence; marketplace commission/escrow/platform payments processing; complex creator discovery; public diagnosis/health; predictions of views/followers/virality; course system; complex application status tracking; competitor history timeline.

## Compliance guardrails
Official Meta/IG APIs only. Graceful degradation with explicit data-availability messaging. Follow-gating only where API/policy permits. Embeds/permalinks for competitor content — never re-host.
